According to Mexico's Defense Ministry, as reported by Mexico News Daily, the operation took place on November 20, 2026.
Elite National Guard special forces known as the "Lobos" and Army special forces known as the "Murciélagos" moved on a house in Guasave, a coastal farming town in northern Sinaloa.
The ministry described three phases. First, they approached by several roads, using stealth and surprise.
Second, they set up a perimeter so nobody could escape or come to help.
They were met with gunfire and explosives, so they went into the house.
Third, they flew him to Mexico City on an Air Force plane.
The ministry said the operation was coordinated between Mexican and U.S. intelligence agencies.

Shortly after 9 p.m. on November 20, Sinaloa's security ministry said federal forces had been attacked in Guasave and told residents to stay indoors.
What followed, Mexico News Daily and HNGN report, was a night of gunfights and so-called narco-blockades.
