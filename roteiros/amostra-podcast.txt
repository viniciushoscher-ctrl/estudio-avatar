Most people over sixty make the same mistake every night, and in Japan, we stopped making it a long time ago.
In Okinawa, the old people say three words before every meal. Hara hachi bu.
It means, stop eating when you are eighty percent full. Not full. Eighty percent.
My grandmother did this her whole life, and she lived to ninety eight.
I am Hiroshi, and tomorrow I will show you the second habit.
