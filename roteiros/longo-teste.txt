Hi, my name is Hiroshi, and I live in Japan. Today, I want to share three small habits that keep people here healthy, even at my age.
The first one is called hara hachi bu. It means you stop eating when you are eighty percent full. Not one hundred percent. Eighty.
In Okinawa, the old people say this before every meal. It sounds simple, but your stomach needs time to tell your brain it is full.
The second habit is walking after dinner. Just fifteen or twenty minutes, slowly, with your family or alone. It helps your body, and it helps your mind.
And the third one, my favorite, is a hot bath every night. We call it ofuro. Warm water, no phone, ten minutes. You will sleep like a baby.
Try just one of these this week, and tell me in the comments how you feel. See you next time, my friend.
